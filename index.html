About me

I’m Khalisa, based in Auckland and currently a second-year student at Auckland University of Technology (AUT). I’m studying a Bachelor of Computer and Information Sciences (BCIS), majoring in Data Science, with a double minor in Mathematical Modelling and Computation, and Finance.

I love turning messy data into something clear and useful, and I’m building my skills with each course and project. Outside of study, I joined AUT STEM Women as a photographer in September 2026, where I’m developing my photography, communication and teamwork skills while supporting the club’s events.

Technology, quantitative finance and creative projects all pull me in – and I like how they feed one another.
